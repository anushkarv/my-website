What I've Been Up To Recently

In this past year, I am so grateful to have met so many incredible people across physics, seismology, and planetary science disciplines! I gave a talk on ground-motions in the Mendocino Fracture Zone at the SSA 2026 conference in Pasadena, met so many inspiring young women at the APS CU*iP conference in Denver, and collaborated with other researchers at the CRESCENT ground motion modeling workshop in Eugene. I am grateful for my advisors at the U.S. Geological Survey and the APS organizers that helped fund me to attend these amazing opportunities. Over the summer, I began my internship with the Militzer group at UC Berkeley, conducting high-pressure computational materials research using quantum mechanical tools, and have enjoyed the rigorous physics based approaches the work entails. More updates to come!
